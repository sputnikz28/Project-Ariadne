# Locations — Gazetteer

Detailed entries per place. See `geography.md` for how these fit together regionally, `canon_index.md` for ids.

**`place:biblioteca_eterna`** — Biblioteca Eterna. The narrative-layer hub. Ariadne's domain; home to the Librarians, the Scribes, and the Chaos Cartographers' published books. Physically hosts the Cripta Eterna (beneath) and the Torreão de Pedra (above).

**`place:elarion`** — Elarion. The realm/city shared by the Kors (native) and the Vampires (whose Cripta Eterna sits within or near it). See `relationships.md` for how this geography shapes their rivalry.

**`place:cripta_eterna`** — Cripta Eterna. Windowless by design ("a frequent triple shines in the mind without needing light"). Home of the two Vampire lineages, Sanguínea and Sombria.

**`place:torreao_de_pedra`** — Torreão de Pedra. The tallest structure in the narrative layer. The Gárgulas occupy its battlements, motionless by day, active when the Council convenes.

**`place:cidadela_de_nemerion`** — Cidadela de Nemerion. Reachable, narratively, only when the Axiomantes' Labyrinth portal opens. Its true geography is combinatorial: 139,838,160 chambers.

**`place:fortaleza_das_montanhas`** — Fortaleza das Montanhas. Seat of the three Dwarf clans (Barbas de Ferro, Cristal Azul, Forja Negra), each with its own king.

**`place:jardim_eterno`** — Jardim Eterno. Home of the Fadas Lunélia.

**`place:floresta_da_lua_cheia`** — Floresta da Lua Cheia. Home of the Lobisomens de Fenrir; most alive during the full moon weeks.

**`place:floresta_ancestral`** — Floresta Ancestral. Home of the Treefolks; they test hypotheses here rather than declare truths.

**`place:laboratorio_genetico`** — Laboratório Genético. Home of the Melforks — implied to be a literal genetic offshoot of the Clerics lineage.

**`place:catacumbas_numericas`** — Catacumbas Numéricas. Home of the Skeletons, arrived alone in Era VI with no cohort.

**`place:ordem_do_tempo`** — Ordem do Tempo. Less a fixed location than a standing order; home of the Cronomantes, thematically linked to Aion.

**`place:templo_dos_clerigos`** — Templo dos Clérigos. The oldest site in the narrative layer (Era I). Where the Ritual Celeste takes place, producing the "Escolha Humana Consagrada pelos Clérigos" from the energy of souls on the Path of the 1000 Souls.

**`place:circulo_do_carvalho_eterno`** — Círculo do Carvalho Eterno. Home of the Druids (Nature Mystics).

**`place:templo_da_lua_prateada`** — Templo da Lua Prateada. Home of the Moon Priests.

**`place:observatorio_de_vidro_celeste`** — Observatório de Vidro Celeste. Home of the Star Gazers.

**`place:tendas_do_vento_ancestral`** — Tendas do Vento Ancestral. Home of the Shamans.

**`place:caldeirao_das_encruzilhadas`** — Caldeirão das Encruzilhadas. Home of the Witches.

**`place:torre_dos_olhos_abertos`** — Torre dos Olhos Abertos. Home of the Seers.

**`place:salao_dos_espelhos_silenciosos`** — Salão dos Espelhos Silenciosos. Home of the Oracles.

**`place:fossa_dos_ossos_sagrados`** — Fossa dos Ossos Sagrados. Home of the Bone Readers.

**`place:paris`** — Paris, França (`Europe/Paris`). The real-world layer: the actual Euromillions draw location and timezone. See `geography.md`, "Two layers, one world."

**Unmapped (canon gap):** the Esquadrão Negro and the Ordem Élfica have no named home yet. Do not invent one without first updating `canon_index.md`.
