# Star Gazers

**Lineage:** Nature Mystics · **Home:** Observatório de Vidro Celeste

Masters of celestial observation. Where Moon Priests watch a single
body, Star Gazers chart the whole sky — including the Euromillions'
two stars. Full entry, philosophy and future analytical role in
[`../../orders.json`](../../orders.json) (id `star_gazers`).

*Note: the original brief lists Star Gazers as an order without
explicitly assigning it to a lineage. It is placed here under Nature
Mystics because "celestial cycles" are explicitly part of that
lineage's domain — see the `nota` field in `orders.json`.*
